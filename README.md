## About Me

I started in systems administration and networking, then moved to the cloud. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the Catholic University of Eastern Africa in Nairobi, and I have worked as a freelance Cloud & DevOps engineer since 2024.

I treat infrastructure as a product: secure by design, defined in code, and built to scale. My projects follow the same routine: set a budget first, build with Terraform, test against real numbers, keep the evidence, then tear everything down.

Security runs through all of it: least-privilege IAM, encryption, zero trust networking and audit trails. I hold three AWS certifications (Solutions Architect Associate, SysOps Administrator, Security Specialty) and CompTIA Security+, and I am working toward CKA and CCNA.

Outside of cloud engineering, 16+ years of classical music training and sports shape how I work: discipline, attention to detail and long-term focus.
